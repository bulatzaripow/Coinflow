# Coinflow


**Track. Understand. Optimize.**  
Coinflow is a personal cash flow manager for iOS that helps you keep your finances clear and under control.

## Features
- Track income and expenses
- Simple and clean transaction management
- Categories for better organization
- Insights into spending habits
- Fast and intuitive iOS-first experience

## Philosophy
Coinflow is built around simplicity.  
No bank connections, no unnecessary complexity — just a clear view of your money.

## Privacy
Your data is your own.
- All data is stored locally on your device
- No ads, no tracking
- No data is shared with third parties

## Tech Stack
- Swift & SwiftUI
- Local data storage
- iOS native design

## Status
Coinflow is under active development.  
Feedback and ideas are always welcome.
